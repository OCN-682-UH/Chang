Cross Referencing

One of Quarto’s biggest advantages for scientific writing: figures and tables are auto-numbered and linkable from your prose. Step 1: Give your chunk a label starting with fig-

Step 2: Reference it anywhere in your prose with Figure 1

As shown in Figure 1, bill dimensions differ clearly by species.

Cross-references:Tables

penguins |>
  group_by(species) |>
  summarise(mean_bill = mean(bill_length_mm, na.rm = TRUE)) |>
  kbl()

Mean bill lengths are summarised in Table 1.

Prefix rules

  • Figures must use a fig- prefix — label: fig-my-plot
  • Tables must use a tbl- prefix — label: tbl-my-table
  • Quarto will error if the prefix doesn’t match the cross-reference type
