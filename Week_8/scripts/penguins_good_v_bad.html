Good Plot

Show code 
good<- ggplot(data=many_penguins,
       mapping=aes(x=genus,y=wing.length, color=genus))+
  labs(title="Penguin Wing Length among Genus", x="Genus",y="Wing Length (mm)")+
  geom_boxplot()+
  guides(color="none")+
  theme_bw()


ggplotly(good) #converts ggplot to plotly

The wing length among various penguin species within the six genus (Aptenodytes, Eudyptes, Eudyptula, Megadyptes, Pygoscelis, and Sphenicus) are represented in Figure 1.

What’s good about this plot?

Clarity

  • Presents raw data
  • Not too many points, variables, or series
  • No legend
  • Simple and organized
    • Emphasis put on the six genus, instead of each species
  • No special symbols or lines

Aesthetics

  • Not cluttered
    • No unnecessary elements
  • Thoughtful color schemes
  • Simple and legible fonts

In Figure 2, we can have a very hard time interpreting what is going on for a number of reasons… This plot seems to compare wing and tail across a number of penguin species.

What’s bad about this plot?

Clarity

  • There are too many data points
  • The legend is overwhelming
  • Nothing is put into context
  • The title is vague and unclear
  • There is an unnecessary trend line

Aesthetics

  • Very cluttered
    • Boxes are too large and close together
  • The coloring is distracting and confusing
    • Grid color and species colors
  • Distracting and large font
    • Title font and legend font don’t match
